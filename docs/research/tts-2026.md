# Text-to-Speech for European Portuguese (pt-PT) — 2025–2026 state of the art

Research date: 2026-09-02. For the fala tutor (currently: OpenAI `tts-1`, Azure `pt-PT-FernandaNeural`, Piper `pt_PT-tugão-medium`).

Sources: primary model cards and vendor docs fetched and read directly (Hugging Face model cards, GitHub READMEs, learn.microsoft.com, platform.openai.com/docs, ai.google.dev, cloud.google.com, elevenlabs.io/docs). Community evidence via SERP only where noted. Prices are vendor-listed at research time; verify before committing.

## TL;DR

- Native pt-PT support is still rare. Most multilingual models ship a generic `pt` that is **trained mostly on Brazilian data** and audibly Brazilian.
- The only genuine pt-PT options found: **Azure pt-PT voices** (4–5, cloud), **Chatterbox-Multilingual-pt-pt** (open, MIT, GPU-oriented), **Piper tugão-medium** (open, CPU, but 1 speaker / ~1.5 h of training data), **Google Cloud pt-PT-Wavenet** (dated), and community XTTS-v2 pt-PT finetunes (non-commercial license).
- Best quality-per-euro for a learner app: **Azure Neural pt-PT** at $16/1M chars, with `gpt-4o-mini-tts` (accent-steerable, ~$15/1M chars effective) as a naturalness upgrade.
- Biggest surprise: Google's **Chirp3-HD and Gemini TTS have no pt-PT** (pt-BR or generic `pt` only). ElevenLabs *claims* "Portuguese (Brazil, Portugal)" for v2 but community reports say you get Brazilian accent unless you pick a pt-PT library voice.

## 3. Recommendation for fala

Learner need: **slow, clear, natural European Portuguese** — pedagogical clarity beats expressiveness, and dialect purity matters more than for media use.

1. **Primary: Azure Neural pt-PT.** Keep/expand it. `pt-PT-FernandaNeural` + `pt-PT-DuarteNeural` are natively pt-PT, $16/1M chars, free 500 K chars/mo tier, SSML `<prosody rate="-20%">` gives reliable slow speech. Evaluate `pt-PT-Rui:MAI-Voice-2` as the premium expressive voice (region + cost permitting).
2. **Secondary: `gpt-4o-mini-tts`** with explicit instructions ("European Portuguese, spoken slowly and clearly, Lisbon standard pronunciation") for conversational turns where naturalness matters. Cost-neutral vs tts-1 (which fala can retire — tts-1 adds nothing gpt-4o-mini-tts lacks except streaming stability).
3. **ElevenLabs: skip** for this use case. 10× the cost and still Brazilian-accent-prone without curated voices.
4. **Offline fallback: keep Piper tugão-medium**, but treat it as degraded mode. It is genuinely pt-PT yet trained on ~1.5 h of one speaker — noticeably weaker than everything cloud-side.
5. **OSS experiment worth one spike:** `ResembleAI/Chatterbox-Multilingual-pt-pt` (MIT, 0.5 B). Try it with a slow, clear pt-PT reference clip. If GPU inference is available it could replace Piper as the local option; it is not CPU-viable for interactive use.

## 4. Known pt-PT quality gaps (the Brazilian-accent problem)

- **Training data asymmetry:** pt-BR has hundreds of millions of speakers of TTS-grade data; pt-PT corpora are tiny (Piper's tugão ≈ 1.5 h single speaker). Every "Portuguese" in Kokoro, XTTS-v2, Parler (via CML-TTS/MLS), F5 finetunes, ElevenLabs, OpenAI, and Gemini defaults toward Brazilian phonetics: open vowels, tapped final syllables, lack of European vowel reduction and full /ʃ/-style palatalization, wrong rhythm (pt-PT is more vowel-reduced and stress-timed).
- **Generic `pt` tags hide dialect:** XTTS-v2, Parler, fish-speech, Eleven v3, Gemini all expose one tag. There is no `pt-PT` switch — the accent depends on speaker/data mix, not a controllable parameter.
- **Steerable-but-unverified:** gpt-4o-mini-tts accent instructions and ElevenLabs voice choice help, but adherence is inconsistent and no vendor publishes pt-PT evals. Adversarial check: reddit r/ElevenLabs threads (1eshop5, 1bnh1vk, 187f1vl) document pt-BR fallback; XTTS-v2's Brazilian bias is stated on the pt-PT finetune card itself.
- **Chirp3-HD / Gemini / Orpheus / MeloTTS / CSM**: simply no pt-PT (or no Portuguese at all) despite multilingual marketing.
