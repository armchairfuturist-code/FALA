# STT for European Portuguese — State of the Art (researched 2026-09-02)

Context: fala currently uses local `openai-whisper` **base** (`whisper.load_model(STT_MODEL)` re-loaded every
call) with an OpenAI-compatible cloud fallback (`whisper-1` / `whisper-large-v3-turbo` via Groq).
This report evaluates what to use for short, accented, mistake-filled learner utterances in pt-PT.

**Adversarial caveat:** most numbers below are measured WER on FLEURS / MLS / CoVoST2 / Common Voice from the
NVIDIA Canary-v2/Parakeet-v3 paper (arXiv:2509.14128) and vendor docs. They are read-aloud-script corpora;
real learner speech with pauses, errors and accents runs 1.5–3x worse. Where a number is marketing or
unverified, it is flagged.

---

## 1. Whisper variants (pt-PT)

Measured FLEURS pt WER (arXiv:2509.14128, Table 11; FLEURS "Portuguese" likely mixes variants — MLS pt is
definitely Brazilian, which flatters models with Brazilian-heavy data):

| Model | Params | pt WER (FLEURS) | pt WER (MLS) | Speed (vs openai-whisper) | Hardware |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| whisper tiny | 39M | poor (not measured here) | — | 10x | CPU, ~1 GB |
| whisper **base** (current default) | 74M | poor (see below) | — | 7x | CPU, ~1 GB |
| whisper small | 244M | fair | — | 4x | CPU, ~2 GB |
| whisper medium | 769M | good | — | 2x | GPU or slow CPU |
| whisper large-v3 | 1.55B | **5.96%** | 3.72% | 1x | ~10 GB VRAM fp16 |
| whisper large-v3-turbo | 809M | ≈ large-v3 for pt (minor degradation) | — | ~8x faster than large-v3 | ~6 GB VRAM; CPU int8 usable |
| **faster-whisper** (CTranslate2) | same weights | identical (same weights) | — | ~4x openai-whisper, int8 CPU-friendly | 2–6 GB |
| whisper.cpp (GGML) | same weights | identical | — | good on CPU/Metal, quantizable | 1–8 GB |
| distil-whisper (official) | — | **NOT USABLE** — English-only (confirmed by HF maintainers) | — | — | — |
| freds0/distil-whisper-large-v3-ptbr | community, **pt-BR** fine-tune | 8.2% on CV-16 (pt-BR) | — | fast | small |

**Key point on `base`:** it is a multilingual model with most capacity spent on English. For Portuguese,
community evaluations put base/small an order of magnitude above large-v3's error rate; Whisper's own paper
shows per-language WER for multilingual models degrades steeply below the medium tier for non-English.
There is **no serious published pt-PT case where base beats small, let alone turbo**. For a tutor whose
entire feedback loop depends on hearing the learner's errors correctly, base is the weakest link.

Implementation notes for fala specifically:
- `speech_to_text()` calls `whisper.load_model(STT_MODEL)` **on every utterance** — model reload each turn.
  Cache it at module level.
- `language="pt"` is correctly pinned — keep this (auto-detect on 10 s clips sometimes guesses Spanish,
  which silently corrupts pt text).
- 10 s fixed recording includes silence → direct hallucination trigger (see §4).

## 4. Hallucination / robustness on learner speech

Directly relevant to fala (short clips, learner mistakes, non-native accent, silence):

- **Non-speech audio is the main trigger.** Barański et al. 2025 (arXiv:2501.11378) built a "bag of
  hallucinations" from non-speech-induced Whisper hallucinations; known silent-audio hallu corpus of 135+
  repeated phrases (e.g., "Thank you for watching", "Subtitles by…") circulates widely. Whisper emits
  these during silence, breathing, keyboard noise.
- **Mitigations that apply to fala today:**
  1. Trim silence with a VAD (silero-vad or webrtcvad) before transcribing; do not feed 10 s of fixed
     recording — record until end-of-speech.
  2. Pass `condition_on_previous_text=False` (short independent clips anyway) and `no_speech_prob` /
     `logprob` threshold checks; drop results below threshold instead of feeding hallucinated text to the LLM.
  3. `temperature` fallback schedule is fine, but prefer re-record over low-confidence guesses.
- Distil-Whisper is *less* prone to long-form hallucination (Calm-Whisper, arXiv:2505.12969) — but English-only.
- Accented/non-native speech degrades all of these; Whisper's hallucination rate rises with accent mismatch.
  The Parakeet/Canary models explicitly added non-speech audio to training to cut hallucinations
  (arXiv:2509.14128) — an advantage over vanilla Whisper for a tutor.
- Parakeet v3 / Canary v2 will **not** auto-correct learner errors, but neither does Whisper; both
  transcribe what was said — what a tutor needs.

## Recommendation for fala

1. **Local whisper `base` is NOT adequate for pt-PT.** It is the cheapest thing that runs, not the cheapest
   thing that works — a tutor that mis-transcribes the learner cannot give correct feedback, and `base`'s pt
   WER is many times worse than turbo/small.
2. **Primary move (low effort, big win):** replace `openai-whisper` with **faster-whisper**, cache the model
   once, and default to `small` int8 on CPU or `large-v3-turbo` int8 if ≥6 GB VRAM is available. Both are
   drop-in for the current record→transcribe loop; `small` is already a large pt-accuracy upgrade over `base`,
   `turbo` ≈ large-v3 accuracy at 8x speed.
3. **If a GPU is available and you want the best open pt-PT model:** **Parakeet-TDT-0.6B-v3** — European
   Portuguese in training, auto language ID, punctuation, timestamps, hallucination-resistant training data,
   CC-BY-4.0. Requires NeMo (heavier dependency) — ponytail: only worth it if faster-whisper-turbo proves
   insufficient.
4. **Cloud fallback:** keep Groq `whisper-large-v3-turbo` ($0.04/hr — cheapest reasonable option). Optional
   second fallback: gpt-4o-mini-transcribe ($0.18/hr) for transcribe-quality insurance; avoid
   gpt-4o-transcribe as primary (community reports of dropped text/latency regressions).
5. **Hallucination hardening regardless of model:** VAD trim before STT, `condition_on_previous_text=False`,
   no_speech/logprob thresholds, record-until-silence instead of fixed 10 s.
6. Skip: Moonshine (no pt), Kyutai STT (no pt), distil-whisper (English-only), MMS (weaker), SeamlessM4T-v2
   (frozen, non-commercial license).
